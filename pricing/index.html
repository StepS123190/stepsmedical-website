Pricing

Medical animation pricing, by production tier

Tiers are based on production complexity and visual sophistication rather than runtime alone, giving flexibility when scoping each project.

Get a quoteSee our process
Production tiers
Three ways to scope your project
Tier 01

Essential 2D

$6,000–$8,000

A clean, cost-effective approach using 2D motion graphics, diagrams, typography and simple illustration, with 1–2 3D still renders where needed, depending on product complexity. Clear, clinical and easy to follow.

Ideal for
  • Medical education
  • Product presentations
  • Simple mechanisms of action
  • Internal communications
  • Conference and digital content
Tier 02

Advanced 3D / Hybrid

$10,000–$15,000

2D motion graphics combined with custom 3D animation, so key anatomy, products or mechanisms can be visualised in greater depth and detail.

Ideal for
  • Product explainers
  • Mechanism of action (MOA) films
  • Medical device demonstrations
  • Biotech platform overviews
  • HCP and patient education
Tier 03

Premium 3D

$18,000–$25,000+

A fully developed, cinematic 3D production with extensive modelling, texturing, lighting and animation. Highly detailed, photoreal visuals for high-stakes audiences.

+ Delivered in 4 aspect ratios (e.g. 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5), plus cut-downs

Ideal for
  • Complex mechanisms of action
  • Pharma launch campaigns
  • Investor and fundraising films
  • Conference keynotes and booth loops
  • Hero brand films
Included in every tier

Script assistance · 2 rounds of revisions at each stage · High-resolution still frames · One MOA cut-down, if needed · Professional voice-over

All pricing is indicative and excludes GST. Final quotes are based on scope, duration, level of detail, scientific complexity and turnaround. Additional revision rounds, extra cut-downs, translations and alternative formats can be quoted on request.

Our perspective

The right tier is the one that matches your audience, not the biggest budget.

A clear internal training video rarely needs cinematic 3D, and a pharma launch film rarely works as a simple 2D explainer. We’ll recommend the tier that fits your subject and audience, then quote against that scope.
