Pharmaceutical Animation

Pharmaceutical animation for treatments, mechanisms and drug delivery

Scientifically grounded 2D and 3D animation for product launches, medical education, investor communication and conferences.

Discuss a pharmaceutical animationSee our work
Steps Medical creates scientifically grounded pharmaceutical animation that explains how therapies work, how they are delivered and what makes them different. We help pharmaceutical, drug-delivery and healthcare teams communicate with clinicians, investors, partners and patients.

Show what cannot be filmed

Drug interactions, biological pathways and treatment effects often happen at scales or locations that live action cannot capture. Animation can move from the whole body to a tissue, cell or molecular interaction, revealing the relationship between the therapy and its intended effect.

The visual approach can be highly cinematic, diagrammatic or a carefully controlled combination of both.

Mechanism of action animation

A strong mechanism of action video gives the viewer enough biological context to understand the intervention, then focuses attention on the key interactions and downstream effect. It avoids unnecessary visual complexity while preserving the distinctions that matter.

We can work from approved scientific material, manuscripts, diagrams, presentations and subject-matter-expert input. See our dedicated mechanism of action animation page for more detail.

Drug-delivery and formulation animation

Explain how a formulation protects an active ingredient, reaches the target site, interacts with tissue or improves administration. Animation can compare conventional routes, reveal internal structures and show a sequence across different scales.

Common applications

  • Product and indication launches
  • Healthcare professional education
  • Medical affairs and internal training
  • Investor and partner presentations
  • Conference presentations and booth screens
  • Patient-facing treatment education
  • Website and digital campaign content

Production approach

  • Discovery and scientific review
  • Script structure and claims alignment
  • Visual development and scale planning
  • Storyboard and timed animatic
  • 3D production, 2D graphics and compositing
  • Review with medical, regulatory or technical stakeholders
  • Final versions, subtitles, stills and cutdowns

Relevant experience

  • CuboPharm: cubosome-based sublingual drug-delivery platform.
  • Alterity: therapeutic science and investor communication.
