Case Study: Bupa

Cutting Bupa’s Australian Paralympic Team ambassador film

A campaign film built from real athlete and training footage, with a bold typography system that carries the story of everything it takes to get to the start line.

Steps Medical: project film.

Bupa backs the Australian Paralympic Team as an ambassador partner. Steps Medical edited and finished the team’s campaign film, shaping real training and competition footage from cycling and swimming into one story, carried by a bold on-screen typography system, for Bupa’s digital and social channels.

About the campaign

Bupa is a proud partner and ambassador supporter of the Australian Paralympic Team, backing athletes across cycling, swimming and beyond in the years, months and days that lead up to a Games. The ambassadors film was built to put the audience inside that build-up, not the medal moment itself, but everything an athlete does to get there.

Steps Medical was engaged to cut and finish the campaign film: structuring the real training and competition footage supplied by Bupa and the athletes into a single narrative, and designing the bold, broadcast-ready typography that carries the film’s voice over the top of it.

The challenge

The raw material was athlete-shot and behind-the-scenes footage from very different settings: a velodrome fitting session, a home gym before dawn, a wet-weather road ride, a pool deck warm-up, captured in different formats, lighting and aspect ratios across cycling and swimming. The brief needed all of it to read as one coherent, emotionally building campaign film rather than a stitched-together highlight reel, and needed to land the same way whether it was seen as a full spot or clipped down for social.

Our approach

We built the edit around a repeating “From …” structure: from training trials, from early starts, from aching muscles, from the warm up, so every new piece of footage is introduced by a short, punchy line of type before it plays, giving the audience a throughline no matter how different each clip’s setting or format is. A restrained black-and-white treatment on select training footage isolates the effort and grit of preparation, before the film returns to full colour and Bupa’s brand palette for the closing “We’re with you all the way” sign-off.

What we delivered

  • Full edit of athlete and behind-the-scenes footage into a single campaign film
  • Broadcast-ready animated typography and on-screen copy system
  • Colour treatment and pacing to unify footage shot across multiple settings and formats

Where it’s used

The film supports Bupa’s partnership with and ambassador backing of the Australian Paralympic Team, running across Bupa’s digital and social channels in the lead-up to major competition.
