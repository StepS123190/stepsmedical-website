Case Study — CuboPharm

Making the case for CuboPharm’s rapid-disintegration tablet platform

A partnering video that turns an invisible formulation-science advantage into a clear, visual case for pharmaceutical licensing decision-makers.

About CuboPharm

CuboPharm has developed a globally patented rapid-disintegration oral tablet technology — a formulation platform designed to get more of a drug’s active ingredient where it needs to go, faster, than conventional oral tablets typically manage. The technology is validated and manufacturing-scalable, and CuboPharm’s focus is now on partnering with pharmaceutical companies to bring it to market.

That makes the video’s job fundamentally a partnering pitch: it has to make a formulation-science advantage legible and credible to the pharmaceutical business development and licensing audiences who decide whether to work with a platform technology like this.

The challenge

Tablet disintegration is invisible in normal use — nobody watches a pill dissolve, and the difference between an ordinary tablet and a rapid-disintegration one isn’t something a photograph or a static diagram can show. CuboPharm needed a video that opens with a problem the audience already half-knows (that oral medicines don’t always fully reach their destination), establishes why that’s been accepted as a limitation for decades, and then makes CuboPharm’s different delivery mechanism visually obvious — without overstating claims a formulation-science audience would push back on.

Our approach

The film opens on a single branded tablet and a direct, plainly stated problem statement, then moves into a close-up 3D visualisation of rapid disintegration itself — the tablet breaking down into a fine, fast-dispersing particle field, in contrast to how a conventional tablet behaves. From there it closes on the commercial case: validated technology, scalable manufacturing, global patents and partnering opportunity, laid out as a clean, confident summary aimed squarely at a partnering or licensing decision-maker.

What we delivered

  • A partnering and investor explainer video for CuboPharm’s rapid-disintegration tablet platform
  • 3D visualisation of tablet disintegration and rapid particle dispersion
  • A closing commercial summary covering technology validation, manufacturing scale and IP position

Where it’s used

The video supports CuboPharm’s conversations with pharmaceutical partners and licensees, giving a fast, visual case for the platform ahead of a deeper technical discussion.
