About Vivo Wellbeing

Vivo Wellbeing is a health and wellbeing support program, giving members access to self-managed strategies and services covering general health goals, cancer care support, and getting back to work and everyday life after a health setback. The offering spans several quite different member journeys under one program, which is exactly what the explainer video had to hold together.

The challenge

A wellbeing program like this can feel abstract from the outside — it’s a bundle of services and support pathways, not a single product a member can picture. Vivo needed a video that could walk a member through what the program actually offers — health and wellness goal support, a menu of services they can choose from, condition-specific support such as cancer care, and support returning to work and home life — without it feeling like a dry list of inclusions, and while carrying the regulatory disclaimers a member-benefits program requires.

Our approach

We used a warm, character-led 2D animation style — a single recurring figure moving through each stage of “ups and downs” — paired with a soft, approachable colour palette that keeps a genuinely broad set of services (fitness and diet, digital health tools, cancer care, return-to-work support) feeling like one continuous, human story rather than a features list. Icons and simple objects (a clipboard, an avocado, a phone, a heart) carry each specific service without needing dense on-screen text, so the pacing stays light even while covering a lot of ground.

What we delivered

  • A character-led 2D explainer animation introducing the Vivo Wellbeing program
  • Visual coverage of general wellness goals, digital health tools, cancer care support and return-to-work support
  • A consistent, approachable visual style carrying required program disclaimers

Where it’s used

The video introduces members to the Vivo Wellbeing program and the range of support available to them across their health journey.
