Case Study: Biome Facial Mist

Biome Facial Mist: bringing microbiome science to a retail shelf

A campaign film for DP Dermaceuticals' Biome Facial Mist, combining real photography with 3D visualisation of the Photobiome Factors and hyaluronic acid technology inside the formula.

About DP Dermaceuticals

DP Dermaceuticals is Dermapenworld’s professional skincare line, sold alongside their microneedling devices to clinics and practitioners. Steps Medical has produced two separate animation and film pieces for the brand: Exosomes Technology, a professional-only regenerative skincare and hair care ingredient, and Biome Facial Mist, a consumer-facing hydrating mist, each with its own audience and its own visual language.

The challenge

Exosomes are cellular signalling particles, genuinely microscopic biology that needed to be shown at a scale and level of detail a clinician would trust, covering how the technology supports cellular repair signalling across skin ageing, pigmentation, rosacea and hair follicles. Biome Facial Mist is the opposite kind of brief: a retail hydration product whose story is about the skin microbiome and barrier, aimed at making a scientific ingredient (Photobiome Factors, hyaluronic acid) feel desirable on a bathroom shelf rather than in a lab.

Our approach

For Exosomes Technology, we built a 3D microscopic world: glowing exosome particles, labelled amino acids, growth factors and proteins, and a cross-section of skin and hair follicle biology, which stays visually consistent with DP Dermaceuticals’ branding while carrying real scientific detail for a professional audience. For Biome Facial Mist, we paired real campaign photography of skin and product with clean 3D visualisations of the Photobiome Factors and hyaluronic acid technology inside the formula, so the science supports the beauty story instead of competing with it.

What we delivered

  • A 3D scientific explainer animation for DP Dermaceuticals’ Exosomes Technology
  • A campaign film for Biome Facial Mist, combining real photography with 3D ingredient visualisation
  • Two distinct visual languages: clinical and scientific for Exosomes, warm and consumer-facing for Biome, built from one consistent brand foundation

Where they’re used

The Exosomes film supports DP Dermaceuticals’ professional partner and practitioner channels; the Biome Facial Mist film supports the product’s consumer-facing marketing and retail presence.
