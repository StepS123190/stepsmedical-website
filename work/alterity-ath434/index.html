Case Study: Alterity Therapeutics

Explaining ATH434’s mechanism of action in Multiple System Atrophy

A molecular-level animation that turns iron biology, protein misfolding and oxidative stress into a clear, accurate story for investors and the scientific community.

Discuss a similar projectBack to all work

Steps Medical: project film.

Alterity Therapeutics’ ATH434 is a novel oral therapy in development for Multiple System Atrophy, working by redistributing reactive iron in the brain to reduce toxic protein aggregation. Steps Medical created a mechanism of action animation that makes that molecular story clear for investors and the wider scientific community.

About ATH434

ATH434 is Alterity Therapeutics’ lead drug candidate, an orally administered small molecule in development for Multiple System Atrophy (MSA), a rare, rapidly progressive neurodegenerative disorder with no approved disease-modifying treatments. ATH434 works by redistributing excess reactive iron inside brain cells, which both reduces the misfolding and toxic aggregation of alpha-synuclein protein and mitigates the oxidative stress that iron build-up causes.

In a randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase 2 trial, both ATH434 dose levels showed clinically meaningful efficacy in slowing disease progression, stabilised orthostatic hypotension symptoms, and helped preserve patients’ walking and standing ability, with a safety profile similar to placebo.

The challenge

ATH434’s mechanism sits at the intersection of iron biology, protein misfolding and oxidative stress: three separate, fairly technical concepts that all have to click together for the story to land. Alterity needed a video that could carry that molecular mechanism clearly enough for investors and the broader medical and scientific community, without oversimplifying the science or losing the audience in jargon along the way.

MSA itself is also not widely understood outside movement-disorder neurology, so the video had to establish what the disease actually does to a person before it could explain how ATH434 intervenes.

Our approach

The film moves in stages: first establishing MSA at the level of the person: the brain, the nervous system, the physical symptoms, then zooming into a single neuron to show reactive iron, toxic alpha-synuclein and oxidative stress accumulating together as the actual disease mechanism. ATH434 is introduced at that same molecular scale, visibly rebalancing the iron and interrupting the process, so the mechanism of action reads as a resolution to a problem the viewer has just been shown, not an abstract diagram.

Iron atoms, protein aggregates and the drug molecule itself are all labelled and color-coded consistently throughout, so a non-specialist viewer can follow the same visual logic a scientific reviewer would.

What we delivered

  • A mechanism of action explainer video for ATH434 in Multiple System Atrophy
  • Molecular-level animation of iron redistribution, alpha-synuclein aggregation and oxidative stress
  • Consistent visual language tying the disease mechanism to ATH434’s intervention

Where it’s used

The video supports Alterity’s investor presentations and communication with the broader medical and scientific community, giving a clear, accurate visual reference for how ATH434 works alongside the company’s clinical trial data.
