Case Study: Alcidion

Explaining Miya Precision, Alcidion’s clinical workflow platform

An explainer video that turns a hospital-wide FHIR-events platform into a clear story for hospital decision-makers and the clinical staff who use it every day.

Steps Medical: project film.

Alcidion’s Miya Precision consolidates fragmented patient information, streamlines clinical communication and supports safer, faster decisions across a hospital. Steps Medical created an explainer video that makes that platform, and the everyday problem it solves, clear to a non-technical audience.

About Miya Precision

Alcidion’s Miya Precision is a FHIR-events platform that sits across a hospital’s existing systems, consolidating fragmented patient information into one clear, real-time view. Rather than replacing what a hospital already runs, it works alongside existing IT investments, pulling data together for clinicians at the point of care.

The platform covers a wide span of clinical and operational needs: electronic patient flow and journey boards, virtual care and remote monitoring, bedside observations and assessments, clinical decision support with AI-enabled alerts, and Smartpage messaging that coordinates tasks and communication between staff. For a viewer coming to it cold, that breadth is exactly what makes it hard to explain in a few minutes.

The challenge

Miya Precision solves a genuinely complex problem: disconnected systems, administrative burden on clinicians, delays in patient flow, and the clinical risk that comes with information living in too many places. Alcidion needed a video that could make that problem and its resolution legible to a non-technical audience: hospital decision-makers and clinical staff who don’t think in terms of FHIR standards or system architecture, but do feel the daily friction the platform removes.

The brief also had to hold together several audiences at once: sales conversations with hospital executives, onboarding for new clinical users, and general marketing, without turning into three separate videos.

Our approach

We combined clear interface visualisation with contextual, real-world clinical scenarios such as an emergency department under pressure, a ward round, a bedside observation, so the platform is never shown in the abstract. Each feature is introduced through the moment it actually gets used: a journey board update that reduces a delay, a Smartpage alert that reaches the right clinician, a consolidated patient view that replaces several logins.

Interface animation was simplified and paced deliberately, rather than shown as a raw screen recording, so the story stays about the clinical outcome: safer, faster, less fragmented care, and not about software navigation.

What we delivered

  • A core explainer video introducing Miya Precision and how it works across a hospital
  • Product and feature animation covering patient flow, clinical decision support and Smartpage messaging
  • Shorter feature snippets for onboarding and internal training use

Where it’s used

The video supports Alcidion’s sales conversations with hospital and health-system decision-makers, and doubles as an onboarding resource to help new clinical users understand the platform faster, without needing a live demo every time.
