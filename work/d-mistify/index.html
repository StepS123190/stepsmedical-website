About d.Mistify

d.Mistify is an intraoral photography and data capture device for dental professionals. Its Air and Light Dispersal (ALD) unit pairs a digital light array with a two-speed turbo airstream, aimed through a series of intraoral mirrors, to keep the mirror surface clear of fog while a clinician works, solving a small but constant friction point in restorative dentistry, orthodontics, periodontics and clinical photography.

The product range spans several kits and mirror types built for specific clinical uses: the PRO Data Capture Kit, Mirror Kit, Perio, Ortho and Ortho PLUS variants, with occlusal, site-specific, arch and straight mirror options, all built around the same handheld ALD device.

The challenge

A fogged intraoral mirror is a small, unglamorous problem, but it directly affects the quality of every photo, mirror-assisted procedure and piece of clinical documentation a dentist takes. d.Mistify needed a video that could make a genuinely mechanical, hardware-level advantage (an airstream and light array keeping a mirror clear) visually obvious, and show the breadth of the product range without turning into a dry catalogue of kit variants and mirror shapes.

Our approach

The film opens on the device itself in isolation, a clean 3D product render that establishes what d.Mistify actually is, then shows the airstream and light beam in action, making the fog-clearing mechanism visible in a way a still photo never could. From there it opens into the full PRO Data Capture Kit, and finally an exploded view of the mirror and accessory range, giving viewers a clear sense of the full system without needing a spec sheet.

What we delivered

  • A 3D product explainer video for the d.Mistify ALD (Air and Light Dispersal) device
  • Visualisation of the airstream and light array mechanism in use
  • Kit and accessory range animation covering the PRO Data Capture Kit and mirror options

Where it’s used

The video supports d.Mistify’s product pages and sales conversations with dental practices considering the device for restorative, orthodontic, periodontic or clinical photography use.
