A structured, review-friendly process

Medical and scientific communication needs clear approval points. Our process is built around a small number of defined checkpoints — script, storyboard and animatic — so that scientific and technical accuracy is resolved early, before the more time-intensive stages of animation and rendering begin.

  1. Discovery

    We start by understanding what needs to be explained, who needs to understand it, and what the viewer should take away. This includes the audience, objective, available technical material, deliverables and the people who will need to review the work.

  2. Script and visual direction

    We organise the information into a clear narrative and establish the visual language — how much is 2D versus 3D, how cinematic or diagrammatic the piece should feel, and how the story moves from problem to explanation to outcome.

  3. Storyboard and animatic

    Before detailed production begins, we build a storyboard and a timed animatic with temporary voiceover where required. This is the point to approve sequence, timing and the major messages — changes here are fast; changes after 3D production begins are not.

  4. Production

    We create the assets, animation, lighting, rendering, compositing and motion graphics. Scientific, medical or technical reviewers can be involved at defined checkpoints, keeping feedback focused rather than open-ended.

  5. Review and delivery

    We complete the agreed feedback rounds and supply final masters, plus the versions you need — cutdowns, stills, subtitles, animated logos and formats for presentations, social media or exhibition screens.

Who’s involved

Projects are led directly by Jason, giving you a clear line of communication from the first conversation through to delivery. Additional specialists — writers, voice artists, sound designers, medical illustrators or production partners — are brought in when the scope requires them.

Working with your reviewers

We work with founders, scientists, clinicians, medical advisers, engineers and commercial teams to establish the right level of technical detail. Review is concentrated at the script, storyboard and animatic stages so accuracy is confirmed before detailed 3D production begins.

What we need from you to start

  • A brief, presentation, scientific paper or product information
  • The intended audience and where the content will be used
  • Any approved claims, brand guidelines or regulatory requirements
  • A target delivery date
