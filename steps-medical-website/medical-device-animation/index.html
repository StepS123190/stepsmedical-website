Medical Device Animation

3D medical device animation that shows how your product works

Precise product visualisation for launches, training, investors and healthcare audiences.

Discuss a medical device animationSee our work
Steps Medical — indicative visual, final photography/video to be supplied
Medical devices often need to be understood before they can be seen in action. Steps Medical creates precise 3D device animation that reveals internal components, product functionality, clinical use and in-body interactions for marketing, training, investors and healthcare audiences.

Reveal what traditional video cannot

A camera can show the outside of a device, but it cannot always reveal the internal mechanism, flow path, assembly sequence or interaction beneath the skin. 3D animation gives complete control over the viewpoint, timing, transparency and level of detail.

It can isolate one feature, transition between exterior and interior views, demonstrate a procedure or place the device in a clean clinical context without the limitations of a physical shoot.

Medical device animation services

  • Product beauty animation and launch films
  • Internal mechanism and exploded views
  • Device setup and operation
  • Clinical-use and procedural sequences
  • In-body interaction and anatomical context
  • Manufacturing or assembly explanation
  • Training and onboarding content
  • CAD-based still renders and campaign assets

Working with CAD and technical files

Where suitable CAD data is available, it can provide an accurate starting point. Files still need to be assessed, cleaned, simplified and prepared for animation and rendering. If CAD is unavailable or incomplete, we can model the required components from references, drawings and dimensions.

Before production begins, we confirm which features must be technically exact and which elements can be simplified for communication.

Made for launches, training and sales

One core 3D asset can support a range of outputs: hero animation, shorter feature videos, sales presentations, trade-show loops, onboarding content, website stills and social cutdowns. Planning these uses early helps create a more efficient asset and shot list.

Our review stages

  • Technical briefing and file assessment
  • Storyboard and animatic with placeholder geometry
  • Asset and material approval
  • Animation and camera approval
  • Lighting, rendering and compositing
  • Motion graphics, labels and final delivery

Relevant experience

  • Ansell: healthcare product communication.
  • Dermapenworld: medical treatment and device-related product storytelling.

Show your device clearly

Send us your CAD files, product references, intended use sequence and communication goals. We can advise what needs to be modelled and the most useful deliverables for your launch or training program.
