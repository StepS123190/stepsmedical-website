Featured work

A curated selection of medical, biotech, healthcare and scientific projects, leading with the strongest medical and scientific work.

Alterity — therapeutic and investor communication

Challenge: introduce complex therapeutic science in a form that investors and non-specialist stakeholders could understand.

Approach: combine clear narration, scientific visualisation and carefully paced motion to establish the problem, technology and commercial relevance.

  • Deliverables: full-length animation, investor edit, clinical edit, social snippets and stills.
  • Related service: Biotech animation and investor presentation videos.

CuboPharm — sublingual drug-delivery platform

Challenge: explain the limitations of conventional delivery routes and show how the cubosome-based matrix behaves on oral mucosa.

Approach: use a 2D and 3D hybrid to compare delivery methods, visualise the platform and explain enhanced retention and absorption without overwhelming the viewer.

  • Deliverables: hero animation, supporting cutdowns, stills and animated brand elements.
  • Related service: Pharmaceutical, mechanism of action and drug-delivery animation.

Alcidion — Miya Precision, digital health and clinical workflows

Challenge: communicate a healthcare platform and the complexity it resolves across clinical teams and systems.

Approach: combine interface animation, clear diagrams, typography and human-centred storytelling to make the workflow understandable.

  • Related service: Digital health and clinical workflow animation.
  • Read the full case study →

Ansell — healthcare product communication

Challenge: explain product benefits and technical information with clarity and strong production value.

Approach: use product-focused visualisation and motion design to support commercial and educational communication.

  • Related service: Medical product and device animation.

Dermapenworld — treatment and product animation

Challenge: communicate treatment science and premium product benefits in a visually engaging way.

Approach: combine beauty, medical visualisation and product storytelling while keeping the explanation accessible.

  • Related service: Medical device, treatment and product-launch animation.
