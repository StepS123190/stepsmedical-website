We help medical, biotech and healthcare teams explain complex treatments, technologies, devices and mechanisms clearly. Steps Medical brings together scientific understanding, cinematic 3D animation, graphic design and motion storytelling to create communication that is accurate, engaging and useful.

Medical animation built around the audience

The same scientific idea may need to be explained very differently to an investor, clinician, patient or commercial partner. Before recommending a visual approach, we clarify the viewer’s existing knowledge, the level of technical detail required and the action the communication needs to support.

This audience-first approach determines the script, pace, visual language and balance between 2D and 3D animation.

Our core services

3D medical animation
High-fidelity visualisation for anatomy, medical devices, pharmaceutical products, internal mechanisms, treatments and in-body interactions.

2D medical animation and motion graphics
Illustration, diagrams, typography, interface animation and data-driven graphics for clear, flexible communication.

Mechanism of action animation
Scientifically grounded visual storytelling showing biological pathways, drug interactions and treatment effects.

Medical device production animation
Accurate product visualisation showing how a device is assembled, functions and is used.

Patient and healthcare explainers
Accessible visual communication for conditions, procedures, treatments, health services and public-health messages.

Digital health and clinical workflow animation
Clear explanation of healthcare software, clinical pathways, user roles, information flows and platform benefits.

What is included

  • Discovery and creative direction
  • Script development or script refinement
  • Storyboards and visual treatment
  • Timed animatic with temporary voiceover where required

  • Animation, lighting, rendering and compositing

  • Voiceover, music and sound design where included
  • Delivery formatted for the required screens, presentations and platforms

Our process

Discovery: define the audience, objective, technical material, deliverables and review team.

Script and visual direction: organise the information into a clear narrative and establish the visual language.

Storyboard and animatic: approve the sequence, timing and major messages before detailed production.

Production: create the assets, animation, lighting, rendering, compositing and motion graphics.

Review and delivery: complete agreed feedback rounds and supply final masters, versions, stills and cutdowns.

Accuracy without losing the story

Medical accuracy and communication clarity are not competing goals. The strongest work respects the source material while guiding the viewer through it in a deliberate sequence. Where appropriate, we collaborate with your scientists, clinicians, engineers and subject-matter experts at defined approval points.

Talk to us about your project

Send through a brief, draft script, presentation, scientific paper, product information or even an early idea. We can help determine the most effective scope and visual approach.
