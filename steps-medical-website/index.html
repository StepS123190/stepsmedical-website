Steps Medical creates high-quality 2D and 3D animation for biotech, pharmaceutical, medical device, digital health and healthcare organisations. We turn complex science, products and clinical workflows into clear visual stories for investors, customers, healthcare professionals and patients.

Explain what matters — clearly and accurately

Medical innovation can be difficult to communicate. A new therapeutic platform, diagnostic technology, medical device or digital health workflow may be technically impressive, but its value can be lost when the audience cannot quickly understand how it works or why it matters.

We combine scientific and technical accuracy with strong visual design. The result is animation that feels credible, engaging and easy to follow without reducing important ideas to generic marketing language.

Selected experience

We have created work for organisations including Alterity, Alcidion, Ansell, CuboPharm, Dermapenworld, Vivo Wellbeing and Bupa. Every project is shaped around the audience, scientific material and communication goal rather than forced into a standard animation template.

A clear production process

We begin by understanding what needs to be explained, who needs to understand it and what the viewer should take away. From there, we help shape the script and visual direction, create storyboards and an animatic, then move into animation once the structure is approved.

Scientific, medical and technical reviewers can be involved at the right stages, keeping feedback focused before detailed production begins. Final deliverables can include a hero video, cutdowns, still frames, subtitles, animated logos and versions for presentations, social media or exhibition screens.
