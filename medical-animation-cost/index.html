Pricing Guide

How much does medical animation cost in Australia?

Medical animation pricing depends on length, complexity and whether the work is 2D, 3D or a hybrid. Here’s what actually drives the cost.

Get a quoteSee our services
There is no single answer to “how much does medical animation cost”. Length, complexity, and whether the work is 2D, 3D or a hybrid all change the number. This guide sets out the real cost drivers, indicative ranges and what’s included, so you can scope your project with confidence before requesting a formal quote.

What drives the cost of medical animation

There is no single price for medical animation because the work itself varies so much. The main cost drivers are:

  • Length and scope: a 30-second cutdown costs far less to produce than a 3-minute hero video.
  • 2D versus 3D: high-fidelity 3D visualisation (devices, cellular environments, molecular interactions) generally takes longer to build than 2D illustration and motion graphics.
  • Complexity of the subject: a single mechanism explained simply costs less than a multi-stage process with several interacting systems.
  • Number of deliverables: a hero video plus cutdowns, stills, subtitled versions and platform-specific formats adds production time beyond the core animation.
  • Voiceover, music and sound design: professional voice talent and sound design are typically quoted separately.
  • Review rounds: most projects include a set number of feedback rounds at each stage; additional rounds beyond that are quoted separately.

Indicative ranges

These are general starting points, not fixed prices: every quote is scoped to the specific project.

  • Under $10,000: short 2D explainers, single-concept animations, simple motion graphics.
  • $10,000–$25,000: a typical 2D or hybrid 2D/3D piece of 60–90 seconds with moderate scientific or technical complexity.
  • $25,000–$50,000: higher-fidelity 3D animation, longer-form pieces, or projects with several deliverables and multiple review stages.
  • $50,000+: flagship investor or launch videos, complex multi-mechanism explainers, or projects requiring extensive scientific visualisation.

What’s included in a quote

  • Discovery and creative direction
  • Script development or refinement
  • Storyboards and visual treatment
  • Timed animatic with temporary voiceover where required
  • 2D and/or 3D production, lighting, rendering and compositing
  • An agreed number of feedback rounds at each stage
  • Final delivery in the formats you need

Payment stages

Projects are typically booked once an agreed upfront payment has been received, with the balance staged across key milestones, for example on storyboard/animatic approval and on final delivery. We’ll set out the exact schedule in your quote.

Get an accurate quote

The fastest way to a real number is to tell us what needs to be explained: the subject, the audience, roughly how long the video should be, where it will be used and your target delivery date. We’ll recommend the right combination of 2D, 3D and motion design and quote against that scope.
