About Steps Medical

Steps Medical is the specialist medical and scientific animation arm of Studio Steps, founded by Melbourne-based Creative Director and animator Jason James.

Steps Medical: indicative visual, final photography/video to be supplied
Steps Medical is the specialist medical and scientific animation arm of Studio Steps, founded by Melbourne-based Creative Director and animator Jason James. We help organisations communicate complex science, technologies, products and clinical workflows through clear 2D and 3D animation.

Creative experience with a medical focus

Jason brings senior experience across animation, motion design, 3D production and creative direction. His work has supported organisations and brands including Google, Meta, Bupa, Ansell, Alcidion, Alterity, CuboPharm and Dermapenworld.

Steps Medical applies that production experience to the specific challenge of medical communication: making technical material understandable while maintaining accuracy, credibility and a high visual standard.

Small, senior and collaborative

Projects are led directly by Jason, giving clients a clear line of communication from the first conversation through to delivery. Additional specialists such as writers, voice artists, sound designers, medical illustrators or production partners can be brought in when the scope requires them.

This model keeps the core team focused while allowing each project to be built around the right expertise.

How we work

We start with the audience and objective rather than a preferred visual technique. Some ideas need high-fidelity 3D animation. Others are clearer with 2D diagrams, interface animation, typography or a hybrid approach.

We establish the story, review process and technical requirements early. Script, storyboard and animatic approval provide clear decision points before detailed animation begins. See our full process.

Based in Melbourne, working across Australia

Steps Medical is based in Melbourne and works with teams throughout Australia. Projects can be run remotely through video calls and structured reviews, with in-person meetings available in Melbourne when useful.

What we value

  • Clarity: the viewer should understand the central idea and why it matters.
  • Accuracy: scientific and technical details are reviewed at the appropriate stages.
  • Craft: design, motion, lighting, sound and pacing should support the message.
  • Collaboration: clients and subject-matter experts need a clear, efficient way to contribute.

Practical delivery: final content should work across the real presentations, screens and platforms where it will be used.
