Investor Presentation Videos

Investor presentation videos for biotech and healthcare fundraising

Clear, credible video that helps investors and partners understand your technology, your differentiation and your opportunity.

Discuss an investor videoSee biotech animation
Investor and partnering conversations move quickly, and technical credibility matters as much as polish. Steps Medical builds investor presentation videos that explain your science, your platform and your opportunity clearly enough for a non-specialist audience to act on.

More than a demo reel

When you’re raising capital or speaking with a potential partner, the video needs to do more than look impressive. In a short amount of time it has to establish the problem, explain the technology, demonstrate differentiation and connect the science to a meaningful commercial opportunity.

We build investor presentation videos around the specific narrative of your raise or your partnering conversation, not a generic company showreel.

Where these videos get used

  • Pitch meetings and roadshows
  • Investor decks and data rooms
  • Company websites and partnering platforms
  • Conference and industry-event presentations

What we cover

  • The problem or unmet need, established quickly and clearly
  • How the technology or platform works, at the right level of technical depth
  • What makes it different from existing approaches
  • Evidence, milestones or data that support the opportunity

Built to be reused

One well-planned production can support several outputs: a full-length version for a formal pitch, a shorter cutdown for a roadshow or social platform, still frames for the deck itself, and a silent, subtitled version for data rooms and booth screens.

Working with your existing material

We can work from your pitch deck, technical papers, existing diagrams or a founder interview. Where the platform involves complex biology, chemistry or engineering, we combine scientific visualisation with clear narrative structure so a non-specialist investor can follow the story.

Relevant experience

  • Alterity: therapeutic science and investor-focused communication.
  • Biotech animation for platforms raising capital or seeking partners.

Plan your investor video

Tell us about your raise or partnering timeline, the audience and the key evidence you want to lead with. We can recommend scope and a realistic production schedule.
