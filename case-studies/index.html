Case Studies

Medical animation case studies

A closer look at how we’ve helped biotech, pharmaceutical, medical device and digital health organisations explain complex ideas clearly.

Every project starts with a communication challenge, not a visual style. These case studies set out the problem we were asked to solve, the approach we took, what we delivered and how the work was used — across biotech, pharmaceutical, medical device and digital health organisations.

Alterity ATH434 — therapeutic and investor communication

Challenge: introduce complex therapeutic science and a novel delivery mechanism in a form that investors, clinicians and non-specialist stakeholders could understand and act on.

Approach: combine clear narration, scientific visualisation and carefully paced motion to establish the problem, the mechanism of action, the therapeutic approach and the anticipated clinical impact.

  • Deliverables: full-length animation, an investor edit, a clinical edit, and social and digital snippets.
  • Impact: used across investor presentations, clinical congress content and digital channels to support education and fundraising.
  • Related service: Biotech animation.

CuboPharm — sublingual drug-delivery platform

Challenge: demonstrate a novel, dissolvable microneedle-style drug-delivery platform and explain how it improves on conventional delivery routes.

Approach: use a clear step-by-step visual flow, comparing delivery methods with a 2D and 3D hybrid to explain the cubosome-based matrix and its behaviour on oral mucosa without overwhelming the viewer.

  • Deliverables: full-length scientific animation, conference loop, explainer snippets and animated brand elements.
  • Impact: supported investor updates, partner discussions and scientific presentations.
  • Related service: Pharmaceutical animation.

Alcidion — Miya Precision, clinical workflow communication

Challenge: explain a sophisticated digital health platform and the clinical workflows it streamlines, across a hospital’s clinical teams and systems.

Approach: combine interface visualisation with contextual real-world scenarios to show how the platform supports better decisions at the point of care.

  • Deliverables: an explainer video, product animations, feature snippets and onboarding videos.
  • Impact: used in sales, onboarding and marketing to improve understanding and adoption.
  • Related service: Clinical workflow animation.
  • Read the full case study →

Dermapenworld — treatment and product animation

Challenge: communicate treatment science and premium product benefits in a way that stayed visually engaging without losing scientific accuracy.

Approach: combine elegant macro visuals and skin-science storytelling to communicate the product’s benefits to both professionals and consumers.

  • Deliverables: launch animation, social edits and a website version.
  • Impact: supported product launch, website engagement and brand positioning.
  • Related service: Medical device animation.

Ansell — healthcare product communication

Challenge: explain product benefits and technical information with clarity and strong production value for a healthcare-product audience.

Approach: use product-focused visualisation and motion design to support commercial and educational communication.

  • Deliverables: product animation and supporting educational content.
  • Impact: supported sales, training and marketing communications.
  • Related service: Medical device animation.
