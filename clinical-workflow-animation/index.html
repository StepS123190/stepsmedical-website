Clinical Workflow Animation

Clinical workflow animation for healthcare and digital health teams

Clear animated explanation of clinical pathways, healthcare software and the people who use them every day.

Discuss a clinical workflow animationSee digital health animation
Clinical workflows connect people, systems and decisions across an already complex healthcare environment. Steps Medical creates clear animated video that shows how a workflow works today, what changes with a new platform or process, and why that improvement matters.

Show the workflow, not just the screens

A clinical workflow usually involves more than one system and more than one person: a clinician, a patient, a piece of software and the steps that connect them. A screen recording on its own rarely explains why the workflow matters or what changes for the people using it.

We combine interface animation, clear diagrams, typography and human-centred storytelling so the viewer can follow the workflow end to end: what happens, who is involved, and what improves.

Workflows we can explain

  • Clinical documentation and care planning
  • Referral, triage and patient-journey pathways
  • Decision support and clinician notifications
  • Remote monitoring and virtual care
  • Interoperability between systems and providers
  • Onboarding and change-management for new platforms

For clinical, commercial and investor audiences

A clinical audience may need to see how their day-to-day changes. A commercial or investor audience may need to understand the problem, the market and the differentiation. We shape the content and level of technical detail around the primary viewer while keeping optional versions available for other audiences.

Interface animation without a screen-recording feel

Rather than relying on long software captures, we isolate the interactions that matter, simplify the surrounding interface and combine UI moments with diagrams and real-world context. This keeps attention on the story while staying recognisably connected to the real product.

Relevant experience

  • Digital health animation for platforms, software and clinical journeys.
