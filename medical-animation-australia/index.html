Medical animation for organisations across Australia

Steps Medical is based in Melbourne and works with biotech, pharmaceutical, medical device, digital health and healthcare organisations throughout Australia, from Sydney and Brisbane to Perth and Adelaide, and everywhere in between.

Most of the process runs remotely: discovery calls, script and storyboard review, and animatic approval are all designed to work smoothly over video and structured feedback rounds, regardless of where your team is based.

Sectors we work with nationally

  • Biotechnology and life sciences companies raising capital or engaging partners
  • Pharmaceutical teams launching products or educating healthcare professionals
  • Medical device manufacturers preparing for launch, training or sales
  • Digital health and healthcare software companies explaining their platforms
  • Healthcare organisations and research commercialisation teams

Why organisations work with a Melbourne-based studio remotely

Medical and scientific animation depends more on a clear process and rigorous review than on physical proximity. A structured script, storyboard and animatic process means your scientific, medical and commercial reviewers can contribute from anywhere in Australia, at defined points, without needing to be in the same room.

How remote projects run

  • Discovery call to establish audience, objective and technical material
  • Script and visual direction shared and reviewed online
  • Storyboard and animatic approved via video review
  • Production updates and milestone check-ins as needed
  • Final delivery in the formats your team needs

In-person when it helps

For teams that prefer to meet in person at key stages, we’re based in Melbourne and can arrange in-person sessions there. See our Melbourne page for local details.
