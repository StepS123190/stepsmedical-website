A Melbourne medical animation studio

Steps Medical is based in Melbourne, working with biotech, pharmaceutical, medical device, digital health and healthcare organisations locally and across Australia. Being Melbourne-based means in-person meetings, workshops and reviews are straightforward when your team prefers to meet face to face.

Melbourne’s life-sciences and health-tech community, from biotech companies and medical device manufacturers to hospitals and digital health startups, is a significant part of the work we do.

What local clients get

  • In-person discovery sessions and script workshops when useful
  • Face-to-face storyboard and animatic reviews
  • A studio that understands the local biotech, medtech and healthcare landscape
  • The same structured, review-friendly process we use with clients across Australia

Working across Melbourne’s health and science sectors

From the biotech and research precincts to medical device manufacturers and digital health teams across the city, we help Melbourne-based organisations explain complex science, products and workflows clearly, for investors, customers, healthcare professionals and patients.

Based in Melbourne, working nationally

While we’re proud of our Melbourne base, most of our work happens with organisations across Australia. See our Australia-wide page for how the remote process works for interstate teams.

Relevant experience

We’ve created work for organisations including Alterity, Alcidion, Ansell, CuboPharm, Dermapenworld, Vivo Wellbeing and Bupa, several of them Melbourne-based.

Let’s meet

If you’d like to talk through your project in person, we’re based in Melbourne and happy to arrange a time.
