Biotech Animation

Biotech animation for complex science and emerging technologies

We turn therapeutic platforms, biomarkers, diagnostics and life-sciences research into clear visual stories for investors, partners and conference audiences.

Discuss a biotech animationSee our work
Biotech companies often need to explain years of research in a matter of minutes. Steps Medical creates clear 2D and 3D animation that helps investors, partners, customers and non-specialist audiences understand what a technology does, how it works and why it matters.

Turn technical depth into a clear story

A platform may involve novel biology, delivery technology, biomarkers, assays, manufacturing processes or multiple stages of validation. Listing every detail rarely creates understanding. We identify the essential narrative, establish what the viewer needs to know first and build the explanation in logical visual steps.

The result remains scientifically credible while giving the audience a clear path through the technology.

Biotech subjects we can explain

  • Therapeutic platforms and mechanisms
  • Diagnostics, biomarkers and genomic technologies
  • Drug-delivery systems
  • Cell and gene therapy concepts
  • Research platforms and commercialisation opportunities
  • Preclinical and clinical development stories
  • Manufacturing or formulation processes
  • Disease pathways and unmet need

Animation for fundraising and partnerships

When raising capital or speaking with potential partners, the animation must do more than look impressive. It needs to quickly establish the problem, explain the technology, demonstrate differentiation and connect the science to a meaningful opportunity.

We create videos for pitch meetings, investor decks, data rooms, websites, conferences and partnering presentations. Shorter cutdowns and still frames can extend the same visual system across the wider campaign. See our dedicated investor presentation videos page.

Relevant experience

  • Alterity: therapeutic and investor-focused communication.
  • CuboPharm: sublingual cubosome drug-delivery platform.

Conference and scientific communication

Animation can help a busy conference audience understand the platform before a detailed conversation begins. Content can be designed for narrated presentations, silent booth screens, large-format displays or short digital follow-ups after the event.

A collaborative review process

We work with founders, scientists, medical advisers and commercial teams to establish the correct level of detail. Technical review is concentrated at the script, storyboard and animatic stages so accuracy is resolved before detailed animation and rendering.

Explain your biotech platform with confidence

Send us your deck, draft script, paper, diagrams or current presentation. We can help identify the clearest narrative and recommend an animation scope for your audience and stage of growth.
